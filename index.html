How it plays

Listener boosts

Pick an amount and write a message. Pay a Lightning invoice, which a Cashu mint turns into ecash, or paste ecash you already have. No account, no app.

The boost is split

Recipients come from the feed's podcast:value block. Each share is locked to its recipient and published as a NIP-61 nutzap with the message.

Creators collect

Nutzaps wait on Nostr relays until the creator collects them, in the inbox or any NIP-60 wallet. They can keep the ecash or send it to a Lightning address.

Built for Podcasting 2.0

Splits, the way the feed says

Point it at an RSS feed and an episode. Each valueRecipient gets the best ecash route:

Recipient hasTheir share goes
A nutzap profile, via npub or the NIP-05 behind their lightning addressAs a nutzap, locked to their key
Only a lightning addressOver Lightning, paid from ecash, with the message as a comment
Only a keysend nodeSkipped (mints can't keysend), and the share is spread over everyone else

Open standards, no lock-in

  • NIP-61 nutzaps that any NIP-60 wallet can collect, matching the Hopp player's wallet plan
  • NIP-73 tags, so any client can tie a boost to its episode
  • NUT-18 payment requests for creators who run their own server
  • Nothing custodial: static files, and leftovers go back to the listener
