Fans don't need an account, and boosts are sent from a throwaway key unless the fan chooses to sign.

          
📴
Creators can be offline
Boosts wait as ecash on Nostr relays until you collect them. You don't need a Lightning node or an always-on wallet.

          
⚡
Tiny amounts work
Ecash moves 21 sats as easily as 21,000, so a small thank-you isn't eaten by fees.

Cashu, NIP-61 nutzaps and Podcasting 2.0. Any compatible wallet can collect your boosts, and the code is MIT.

        

      

    


    

      
FAQ
Good questions.

      
What is Cashu ecash?
Cashu is an open protocol for Bitcoin-backed digital cash. A mint issues tokens ("nuts") for sats. They move instantly, cheaply and privately, and can be swapped back to Lightning at any time.

      
Do fans need a wallet or an account?
No account. Fans pay a Lightning invoice from any wallet, or paste Cashu ecash they already have. The sats are turned into ecash and delivered with their message.

      
Is Nutjar custodial?
No. Nutjar is a static web app with no server. Sats are held by the Cashu mint the creator chooses until the creator collects them. Like any mint-based system, you trust that mint for the sats it backs, so pick mints you trust.

      
How do creators get the money out?
Open the creator app, sign in with your Nostr signer, and tap Collect. Keep the ecash in any Cashu wallet, or send it straight to your lightning address.

      
Are boosts public?
Nutzaps are Nostr events, so the amount, message and episode are visible. The sender is anonymous unless they choose to sign with their own Nostr key. For private boosts, creators can use a NUT-18 payment request instead.

      
What does it cost?
Nutjar takes nothing. Mints may charge a small fee per swap, and Lightning payouts include routing fees. Both are shown before you pay.
