How it works

Listener boosts

Pick an amount and write a message. Pay a Lightning invoice, which a Cashu mint turns into ecash, or paste ecash you already have. No account, no app.

The boost is split

Recipients come from the feed's <podcast:value> block. Each share is locked to its recipient (P2PK) and published as a NIP-61 nutzap with the message.

Creators collect

Nutzaps wait on Nostr relays until the creator collects them, in the inbox or any NIP-60 wallet. They can keep the ecash or send it to a Lightning address.

Built for the Podcasting 2.0 world

Splits, the way the feed says

Point the widget at an RSS feed and an episode. It reads the podcast:value recipients (item-level overrides channel-level) and finds each one's ecash route:

Recipient hasTheir share goes
A nutzap profile (kind 10019), via npub or the NIP-05 behind their lightning addressAs a nutzap, locked to their key
Only a lightning addressOver Lightning, paid from ecash (melt) with the message as a LUD-12 comment
Only a keysend nodeSkipped (mints can't keysend), and the share is spread over everyone else

Open standards, no lock-in

  • NIP-61 nutzaps: any NIP-60 wallet can collect them, and they match the plan in the Hopp player's wallet design notes (ADR 0005).
  • NIP-73 tags (podcast:item:guid, podcast:guid), so any client can tie a boost to its episode.
  • NUT-18 payment requests: point the widget at a creqA… and the boost is POSTed straight to the creator's server.
  • Nothing custodial. The widget is static files. Sats go from the mint straight to the creators, and any leftover goes back to the listener as a token.
